ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Give Your Engine The Care It Deserves

Engine oil plays an important role in protecting your motorcycle engine. It helps lubricate moving components, reduce friction and manage engine heat.

Over time, engine oil can lose its effectiveness. Regular oil replacement helps maintain proper engine lubrication and supports smooth performance.

At MotoFix Garage, we inspect your motorcycle's oil condition and use suitable engine oil based on the manufacturer's recommendations.

Complete Motorcycle Oil Change Service

Our oil change service includes essential checks to help maintain your motorcycle's engine health.

Oil Condition Check

Inspect the existing engine oil condition and identify signs of deterioration.

Old Oil Removal

Remove used engine oil as part of the scheduled oil replacement service.

Fresh Engine Oil

Oil Filter Inspection

Oil Level Check

Final Inspection

WHY REGULAR OIL CHANGES MATTER

Benefits of Regular Oil Changes

Timely oil replacement helps protect important engine components and maintain reliable performance.

Engine Protection

Proper lubrication helps protect engine components from excessive friction.

Smooth Operation

Fresh, suitable oil supports smooth operation of engine components.

Heat Management

Engine oil helps carry heat away from lubricated engine components.

Engine Maintenance

Following recommended oil intervals supports long-term engine care.

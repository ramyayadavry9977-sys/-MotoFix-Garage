ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Keep Your Bike's Electrical System Healthy

Your motorcycle's electrical system plays an important role in starting, lighting, charging and overall performance. Even a small electrical fault can affect your daily riding experience.

At MotoFix Garage, we inspect electrical components carefully and help identify problems with batteries, wiring, lights, switches and charging systems.

  • Battery and charging system inspection
  • Wiring and connection checks
  • Headlight and indicator inspection
  • Electrical fault identification
